Controls

W - Thrust forward
A - Turn left
S - Thrust Backward
D - Turn Right
Mouse LMB - Shoot

1 - Choose Minigun if unlocked
2 - Choose Shotgun if unlocked
3 - Choose Missile Swarm if unlocked
4 - Choose Repulser Gun if unlocked
5 - Enable CullFace
6 - Disable CullFace
7 - Choose GL_FILL
8 - Choose GL_LINE
0 - Unlocks all Weapons (Master)

Entities

Asteroids - Vary with sizes and deals damage proportional to its size when collided. Will have random velocity.
Normal Enemy Ship - Flies towards player ship and fires at it in a straight line
Boss - Has two phases: (1) Flies around player while its body fires bullets sideways at different speeds (2) Body breaks away and indiviually flies towards the player and fires at it while the head fires at the ship with a bellowing flame of bullets
       Damage taken will reduce the boss size;
Planet - Vary with sizes and has gravitational pull proportional to its size when collided.
Black Hole - Vary with sizes and has gravitational pull proportional to its size when collided. Avoid at all cost.
Powerups - Blue floating orbs which will float towards the player within a certain distance. The player unlocks a random weapon and restores health when collided but if weapon has already been unlocked, will spawn in a buddy ship which is equipped with a shotgun and will aid the player.
